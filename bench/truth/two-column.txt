Ridge Water Survey - Spring Report
3
Springs of the Eastern Ridge
1. Field notes
The survey team walked the eastern ridge in the
first week of May. They recorded each spring,
its flow in litres per minute, and the colour of
the water. Most springs ran clear. Two ran
brown after rain and cleared again within a day.

2. Equipment
Each team carried a hand pump, a set of sample
bottles, a notebook and a small scale. The scale
drifted by a few grams over the week, so the
team checked it against a reference weight
every morning.

3. Results
The ridge holds forty-one springs. Thirty-six of
them flow all year. The five seasonal springs sit
on the south slope, where the soil is thin and
the rock is close to the surface.

4. Next steps
The team will return in October to measure the
same springs at the end of the dry season. They
will also map the two brown springs and look
for the source of the silt.
